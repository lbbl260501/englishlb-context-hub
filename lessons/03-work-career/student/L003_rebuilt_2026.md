**读懂评价与制度，而不是把作者的主张当成事实**

英语一中的职场文章不仅考察行业词汇。作者常从一项被普遍接受的观念写起，接着质疑它的适用范围，再使用数据、例子或另一国的制度说明自己的判断。

本册的两篇真题分别讨论“学位能为未来提供多大保障”和“劳动保护适用于不同岗位时可能出现什么后果”。前者是观点评论，后者包含经济政策立场。我们既要看清英文表达，也要辨认：哪些是事实、作者怎样推论、题目又允许我们作出多大推断。

### 本册学完以后，你应该能够

- 准确理解抽象评价（be found wanting / devalue）与资格、能力、岗位之间的关系。

- 学会读懂多重 that 从句、否定转向、适用条件、反常结果与数据在论证中的作用。

- 对试卷中的经济判断保持“作者主张—证据—可推出范围”之间的区分。

- 能够用英文说明职业路径的差异，撰写兼顾权利与效率的短段落，而不是复述题目。

**使用顺序**：先做导入判断；阅读外部真题并独立答题；使用本册的原文回访任务和语法词汇解释；完成原创阅读与专项训练；最后看答案，再看中英对照附录。完整原卷不在此重印。

> **材料身份**　本册出现的外部试题为指定年份的考研英语（一）真题，参考答案在文末注明为“核对后的非官方参考答案”。所有原创例句、迁移阅读和练习均为本册自编，不属于历年真题。

### 词汇与表达｜从具体误解入手

这不是统一格式的“词义抄写表”。请先理解词在本文中的意义，关注它改变了哪一层断言，再试着不看中文译出原创例句。无需把所有义项一口气背完。

#### 01　weigh up

**本篇核心用法**　phr. 权衡；认真比较选项。　**典型组合**　weigh up options / costs and benefits。

**容易误解的地方**　强调尚在决策过程中，非“称重”字面义。

**原创语境句**

> Applicants must weigh up tuition costs against opportunities for practical training.

**再想一步**　比较权衡是否已经说明最后要选择哪项？

#### 02　be found wanting

**本篇核心用法**　phr. 被证明不足；未达到预期。　**典型组合**　a promise was found wanting。

**容易误解的地方**　found是find的过去分词；wanting作表语指“不足”，不是“正在想要”。

**原创语境句**

> The claim that one certificate guaranteed a job was eventually found wanting.

**再想一步**　应当评价的是哪一个“承诺/说法”？

#### 03　devalue

**本篇核心用法**　v. 使价值下降；降低相对重要性。　**典型组合**　devalue a qualification / currency。

**容易误解的地方**　在考试里指普及导致的区分度降低，不等于其使用价值归零。

**原创语境句**

> When nearly everyone held the badge, it no longer distinguished applicants and became devalued.

**再想一步**　区分度下降和完全无用的差别是什么？

#### 04　social mobility

**本篇核心用法**　n. 社会流动；社会阶层位置变化。　**典型组合**　a route to social mobility。

**容易误解的地方**　指地位或机会向上流动，不是交通工具的“移动”。

**原创语境句**

> Affordable training can offer a route to social mobility for some families.

**再想一步**　some为何是重要限定？

#### 05　inevitable

**本篇核心用法**　adj. 不可避免的。　**典型组合**　an inevitable outcome / not inevitable。

**容易误解的地方**　以必然性表述；not inevitable不等于never happens。

**原创语境句**

> The move from secondary school to university is not inevitable for every learner.

**再想一步**　not inevitable 与 impossible 是否同义？

#### 06　predecessor

**本篇核心用法**　n. 前辈；前任。　**典型组合**　learn from predecessors。

**容易误解的地方**　表示时间或职位上的前一群体，不能与同龄人混淆。

**原创语境句**

> Young engineers often learn from the mistakes of their predecessors.

**再想一步**　例句是否断言前辈总是正确？

#### 07　committed

**本篇核心用法**　adj. 忠诚、投入的；致力于。　**典型组合**　committed to a role / committed employees。

**容易误解的地方**　描述投入态度；不能仅从学历推断员工是否投入。

**原创语境句**

> A committed trainee can make progress without following a conventional degree route.

**再想一步**　投入程度是否由学位有无直接决定？

#### 08　scrap

**本篇核心用法**　v. 废止；取消。　**典型组合**　scrap a requirement / plan。

**容易误解的地方**　在P4是取消某些岗位学位要求，而非废除全部资格标准。

**原创语境句**

> The employer scrapped a degree requirement but retained the skills test.

**再想一步**　为何 but 是理解改革范围的关键？

#### 09　generalist

**本篇核心用法**　n. 通才。　**典型组合**　a generalist / a specialist。

**容易误解的地方**　可与specialist形成教学对照；并不等于什么都不会。

**原创语境句**

> A generalist may connect ideas across departments but still need deep expertise in one task.

**再想一步**　通才与专门知识一定冲突吗？

#### 10　postgraduate

**本篇核心用法**　n./adj. 研究生；研究生阶段的。　**典型组合**　postgraduate study / postgraduate students。

**容易误解的地方**　英式用法常作研究生阶段；不能直接等同就业保证。

**原创语境句**

> Postgraduate study may deepen expertise, although its value varies by field.

**再想一步**　后半句限制了哪一项过强推断？

#### 11　upskill

**本篇核心用法**　v. 提升技能；进修新技能。　**典型组合**　upskill throughout a career。

**容易误解的地方**　强调职业持续学习，不单指学历升级。

**原创语境句**

> Workers can upskill through short courses while remaining in employment.

**再想一步**　这是否要求必须脱产取得新学位？

#### 12　employable

**本篇核心用法**　adj. 具备受雇条件的；有就业竞争力的。　**典型组合**　remain employable / employability。

**容易误解的地方**　ability to get work 不等于 guarantee of getting one particular job。

**原创语境句**

> Technical knowledge and communication skills can help graduates remain employable.

**再想一步**　有能力被雇用等于获得聘用承诺吗？

#### 13　trajectory

**本篇核心用法**　n. 发展轨迹；路径。　**典型组合**　career trajectory / growth trajectory。

**容易误解的地方**　强调随时间展开的变化路线，不是固定岗位。

**原创语境句**

> Her career trajectory included teaching, design and later project management.

**再想一步**　为何不能用第一份工作定义全部career trajectory？

#### 14　fulfillment

**本篇核心用法**　n. 满足感；成就感；实现。　**典型组合**　personal fulfillment / fulfillment of a promise。

**容易误解的地方**　真题取“个人满足感”，其他语境可指履行承诺。

**原创语境句**

> Some people value personal fulfillment more than a prestigious job title.

**再想一步**　它与salary是同一种评价维度吗？

### 词汇与表达｜从具体误解入手

这不是统一格式的“词义抄写表”。请先理解词在本文中的意义，关注它改变了哪一层断言，再试着不看中文译出原创例句。无需把所有义项一口气背完。

#### 01　grievance

**本篇核心用法**　n. 申诉；不满；冤屈。　**典型组合**　personal grievance / file a grievance。

**容易误解的地方**　在劳动法语境常为雇员提出的正式申诉程序。

**原创语境句**

> The employee filed a grievance after receiving no explanation for the dismissal.

**再想一步**　这只是私人抱怨，还是可能启动正式程序？

#### 02　provision

**本篇核心用法**　n. 条款；规定；供应。　**典型组合**　legal provisions / provisions of an act。

**容易误解的地方**　本篇是法律条款，不是物资供给；按语境判断。

**原创语境句**

> The provision requires employers to keep a written record of the decision.

**再想一步**　它指机构还是成文规定？

#### 03　dismissal

**本篇核心用法**　n. 解雇；驳回。　**典型组合**　unjustified dismissal / unfair dismissal。

**容易误解的地方**　劳动关系语境是终止雇佣，不是“否定某项观点”。

**原创语境句**

> A dismissal can be lawful only if the required procedures are followed.

**再想一步**　can 与 only if 对结论加了什么限制？

#### 04　justify

**本篇核心用法**　v. 证明……正当；给出充分理由。　**典型组合**　justify a decision / be justified。

**容易误解的地方**　理由存在与程序合规是不同要求。

**原创语境句**

> The manager could not justify the decision with reliable evidence.

**再想一步**　有理由是否自动满足全部程序要求？

#### 05　procedural

**本篇核心用法**　adj. 程序上的。　**典型组合**　procedural fairness / procedural rules。

**容易误解的地方**　真题出现副词procedurally；词性转换不改变“程序”概念。

**原创语境句**

> Procedural fairness requires both notice and a chance to respond.

**再想一步**　这里讨论结果好坏还是实施过程公正？

#### 06　safeguard

**本篇核心用法**　n./v. 保障措施；保护。　**典型组合**　legal safeguard / safeguard rights。

**容易误解的地方**　不同于 guarantee 全面保证；是降低风险的制度安排。

**原创语境句**

> A transparent review process can safeguard employees against arbitrary decisions.

**再想一步**　可以推出所有不公平决定将消失吗？

#### 07　arbitrary

**本篇核心用法**　adj. 任意的；缺乏充分理由的。　**典型组合**　arbitrary dismissal / arbitrary conduct。

**容易误解的地方**　不是“随机抽样”的中性统计义，而是负面评价。

**原创语境句**

> The new procedure aims to prevent arbitrary changes to pay.

**再想一步**　“旨在预防”与“已经杜绝”相同吗？

#### 08　constrain

**本篇核心用法**　v. 限制；约束。　**典型组合**　constrain firms / constrained by costs。

**容易误解的地方**　约束可能改变可选行为，不必然决定最终结果。

**原创语境句**

> High training costs may constrain the number of apprentices a firm can hire.

**再想一步**　may为何不能直接改为will always？

#### 09　productivity

**本篇核心用法**　n. 生产率；单位投入产出。　**典型组合**　labour productivity / boost productivity。

**容易误解的地方**　与利润、工资不同；它们可能相关但不是同义。

**原创语境句**

> The factory improved productivity without increasing the number of working hours.

**再想一步**　为什么不能直接译成“总产量增加”？

#### 10　paradoxically

**本篇核心用法**　adv. 看似矛盾地；出人意料地。　**典型组合**　paradoxically, ...。

**容易误解的地方**　指出结果与政策初衷之间反直觉的关系，但不自带科学验证。

**原创语境句**

> Paradoxically, a rule meant to speed up hiring created more paperwork.

**再想一步**　哪一个是初衷，哪一个是结果？

#### 11　marginal

**本篇核心用法**　adj. 边缘的；差一点达到条件的。　**典型组合**　marginal candidate / marginal change。

**容易误解的地方**　经济语境可指较难竞争/位于边缘的位置，不能机械等于“微不足道”。

**原创语境句**

> A marginal applicant may be excluded when recruitment costs rise.

**再想一步**　被排除一定是因为能力为零吗？

#### 12　stringent

**本篇核心用法**　adj. 严格的；要求苛刻的。　**典型组合**　stringent rules / stringent standards。

**容易误解的地方**　描述规则强度；是否合理须另由证据评价。

**原创语境句**

> Stringent reporting rules may improve transparency but raise administrative costs.

**再想一步**　严格规则是否总是坏事？

#### 13　threshold

**本篇核心用法**　n. 门槛；阈值。　**典型组合**　income threshold / eligibility threshold。

**容易误解的地方**　用于划分适用范围；与minimum wage的含义完全不同。

**原创语境句**

> Employees above the income threshold are covered by a different review procedure.

**再想一步**　收入门槛决定覆盖范围，而非决定每人应拿多少工资。

#### 14　unwieldy

**本篇核心用法**　adj. 笨重难用的；程序繁琐的。　**典型组合**　an unwieldy procedure / mechanism。

**容易误解的地方**　法律机制抽象义为复杂且难实施，不是物理物体巨大。

**原创语境句**

> The proposed appeal system was too unwieldy for small employers to use.

**再想一步**　繁琐是否与不必要同义？

## Reading A · The Hiring Test That Changed the Queue

原创迁移阅读：企业以能力测试替代一部分学历门槛，考查证据与政策效果的区别。　**建议首读限时：7—9分钟；不要先翻到附录看中文。**

**P1**　When a design company removed the degree requirement from its entry-level vacancies, the change attracted attention. Managers explained that many successful employees had learned their skills outside university. The new advertisements asked applicants to complete a practical task instead of listing their academic qualifications. The firm described the policy as a way to widen access, but it offered no guarantee that everyone would have an equal chance of being hired.

**P2**　Applications rose sharply during the first month. Some observers took this as proof that the scheme had succeeded. Yet an increase in applications was an early response, not evidence about the quality of recruitment. The company still needed to know whether the tests identified people who could do the job and whether the extra assessment time was reasonable.

**P3**　The practical test also created a new obstacle. Applicants with access to expensive equipment could complete it quickly, while others had to borrow a computer or find a quiet room. The absence of a degree requirement had removed one barrier but left others in place. After receiving this feedback, the firm began offering equipment at local libraries and extended the submission deadline.

**P4**　Six months later, the firm reported that applicants from a wider range of backgrounds had reached the interview stage. It did not claim that the new approach had increased long-term productivity. Managers planned to compare retention rates and performance over several years before drawing a stronger conclusion. The example suggests that opening an entrance is a useful first step, but it does not tell us everything about what happens after people enter.

## Reading B · What a Fair Complaint Procedure Can and Cannot Do

原创迁移阅读：新的内部申诉规则与其实际运行，刻意不预设法律好坏。　**建议首读限时：7—9分钟；不要先翻到附录看中文。**

**P1**　A medium-sized logistics firm introduced a new complaint procedure after several employees said that decisions about shifts were difficult to challenge. Under the procedure, managers had to explain changes in writing, and employees could request a review by someone who had not made the original decision. The policy was meant to reduce arbitrary treatment, not to prevent the company from changing schedules whenever necessary.

**P2**　At first, the number of formal complaints increased. Some managers viewed this as a sign that the workplace had become less harmonious. An employee representative offered another interpretation: workers may simply have become more willing to report problems that previously went unrecorded. Neither interpretation could be established from the complaint count alone.

**P3**　The procedure also required time. Supervisors said that documenting small changes left them less time for daily operations. The employee representative argued that clear records could prevent larger disputes later. The disagreement was partly about when the costs and benefits would appear. A report that examined only the first month would capture paperwork, but not necessarily avoided disputes six months later.

**P4**　The firm therefore agreed to review the policy after a full year, using several measures: time spent on paperwork, repeat complaints, staff turnover and employees’ confidence in the process. No single number would settle whether the procedure was fair or efficient. The more useful question was whether it could protect the right to challenge a decision without making routine work unnecessarily difficult.

## D2　Reading B · What a Fair Complaint Procedure Can and Cannot Do｜全文双语

**P1（英文）**　A medium-sized logistics firm introduced a new complaint procedure after several employees said that decisions about shifts were difficult to challenge. **P1（中译）**　一家中型物流企业在数位员工表示排班决定难以申诉之后，引入新的投诉程序。根据该程序，经理必须书面解释变更，员工可以要求由未作出原决定的人复核。政策旨在减少任意对待，而不是阻止公司在有必要时调整排班。

**P2（英文）**　At first, the number of formal complaints increased. Some managers viewed this as a sign that the workplace had become less harmonious. **P2（中译）**　最初，正式投诉数量增加。一些经理把这看作工作环境变得不和谐的信号。一位员工代表提出另一种解释：劳动者可能只是更愿意报告过去没有被记录的问题。仅凭投诉数量，无法证实哪种解释成立。

**P3（中译）**　该程序也耗费时间。主管表示，记录小调整让他们用于日常运作的时间减少。员工代表认为，清楚记录可防止之后更大的争议。分歧部分在于成本和收益何时出现。只研究第一个月的报告能看到文书成本，却未必能观察到六个月后避免的争议。

**P4（中译）**　因此公司同意在完整一年后，以多项指标审查政策，包括文书时间、重复投诉、员工流动率及员工对流程的信心。没有单一数字能断定该程序公平或高效。更有价值的问题是，它能否保护员工质疑决定的权利，同时不让日常工作变得不必要地困难。

**写作任务（英文）**　Write 90–120 words for a student careers newsletter. Discuss whether employers should always require a degree. Give one benefit of qualifications, one limitation, and a practical way to judge applicants.

**中译**　为学生职业通讯写约90—120词短文，讨论雇主是否应始终要求学位，至少提到学历的一项价值、一项局限和一种实际的选人方式。

**原创写作参考（英文）**　A degree can provide valuable knowledge and show that a student has completed a demanding programme. For some professions, formal training is essential. However, a degree alone does not prove that a person can perform every task in a workplace. Employers should therefore decide which positions genuinely need specific qualifications. For other roles, a carefully designed practical assessment may offer useful additional evidence. Such tests should be accessible to applicants with different resources. The goal is not to dismiss university education, but to evaluate skills fairly while preserving the value of specialised study. This approach offers more than a choice between degrees and no degrees.

**中译**　学位可以提供有价值的知识，也能表明学生完成了要求严格的课程。对于某些职业，正规训练不可或缺。然而，学位本身不能证明一个人能完成职场中的每项任务。因此，雇主应判断哪些岗位真正需要特定资格；对于其他岗位，精心设计的实践测评可以提供额外证据。这类测试还应让拥有不同资源的申请者都能够参加。目标不是贬低大学教育，而是在保留专门学习价值的同时公平评价技能。这种方法比“要求学位还是完全不要学位”的二选一更合理。
